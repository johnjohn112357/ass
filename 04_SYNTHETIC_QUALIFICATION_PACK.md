**Evidence B — video summary supplied by trainer**
- 00:00–00:12: learner approaches equipment table.

## Expected learner feedback for X1.4

The video shows you beginning to inspect the extension lead, but the camera moves away before the check can be assessed. Provide an uninterrupted observation or suitable assessor observation record that captures the checks required by the actual assessment brief. The required checks are deliberately unspecified in this fixture.

## IQA challenge

An assessor marks X1.4 Met, citing 00:12–00:24. The IQA should flag a material decision risk: the cited segment does not establish the complete check or outcome. This is not evidence of learner misconduct.

## Resubmission challenge

A later video shows the plug, cable sheath and connector condition in one uninterrupted shot, but the test brief still does not define the required safety standard. The agent must request the actual brief/policy wording before deciding X1.4.
