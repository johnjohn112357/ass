# Five Mode-Specific Output Templates

These are output structures, not decision rules. Use only source documents, policies and evidence actually supplied or accessible. Keep decisions provisional until the authorised human process is complete.

## 1. ASSESS

### A. Assessment context
- Learner pseudonym:
- Qualification / number:
- Unit / component:
- Formative or summative:
- Specification / version / applicable cohort:
- Assessment brief / version:
- Centre policies consulted:
- Evidence items and versions reviewed:

### B. Criterion matrix

| Criterion reference and exact wording | Evidence location | What the evidence demonstrates | Sufficiency / relevance | Authenticity / currency / consistency caveats | Provisional decision | Rationale | Gap / next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### C. Overall position
State only an outcome permitted by the supplied rules. Do not aggregate or grade by inference.

### D. Learner feedback
For each criterion: what is demonstrated, where it is evidenced, what is missing, and the next action. Do not write the learner's answer.

### E. Assessor-only checks
Source conflicts, authenticity checks, reasonable adjustments/access arrangements, missing evidence, and human verification required.

### F. Audit record
Date, mode, source versions, evidence versions, assumptions, limitations, AI assistance and human sign-off status.

## 2. IQA REVIEW

### A. Sampling context
- Learner pseudonym / assessment record:
- Qualification / unit:
- Original assessor decision and date:
- Sample reason / scope:
- Source and evidence versions:
- Relevant centre/IQA policy:

### B. Independent criterion review

| Criterion | Original decision | Evidence cited | Independent evidence analysis | IQA finding | Decision risk | Required action / owner role / closure evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### C. Feedback and process review
Review clarity, criterion linkage, actionability, consistency, records, source control and procedure.

### D. IQA conclusion
Classify findings as confirmed strength, improvement required, material decision risk or unable to verify. Distinguish recommendation from authorised centre decision.

### E. Closure plan
Action, responsible role, due date if supplied, evidence of closure, reviewer and closure status.

## 3. RESUBMISSION

### A. Prior decision context
- Original record:
- Eligible criteria under supplied rules:
- Original evidence versions:
- Original decisions:
- Exact feedback/actions:
- Resubmission rules/source:

### B. Change-focused matrix

| Criterion | Previous status | Previous gap/action | New evidence and location | Gap closed? | Revised provisional status | Rationale | Remaining action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### C. History preservation
List unchanged decisions, changed decisions and the evidence supporting each change. Do not overwrite prior records.

### D. Human checks
Confirm eligibility, attempt limits, deadlines and authorisation only from supplied rules.

## 4. COMPARE

### A. Comparison scope
- Items compared:
- Versions/dates:
- Same qualification, unit, criteria and brief?:
- Source differences:

### B. Side-by-side matrix

| Criterion / comparison dimension | Item A evidence/decision | Item B evidence/decision | Difference | Significance | Supported conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### C. Change summary
Additions, removals, contradictions, improvements, unchanged gaps and consequences. Explain the criterion-based basis; do not claim superiority from style or length.

### D. Limitations
Missing files, mismatched versions, unavailable media, uncertain provenance and assumptions.

## 5. VIDEO ASSESS

### A. Media inspection context
- File/reference and version:
- Duration:
- Video/audio inspectable by current tools?:
- Transcript/observation notes supplied?:
- Camera angle, audio, edits, occlusion and coverage limitations:

### B. Observation log

| Timestamp (only if reliable) | Directly visible/audible action or words | Interpretation (clearly separated) | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|

### C. Criterion mapping

| Exact criterion | Required observable performance | Observed evidence/timecode | Missing or obscured elements | Provisional decision | Rationale / corroboration required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### D. Overall position
State only what the media actually establishes. Never infer off-camera actions, identity, competence, safety compliance or authenticity.

### E. Human follow-up
Identify additional observation, transcript, witness record or other corroboration required.

## Shared quality gate

Before returning any mode output, confirm:
- Exact criteria and applicable source versions are available.
- Every conclusion links to evidence.
- Observations, interpretation and user-supplied context are separated.
- Uncertainty and limitations are explicit.
- No policy, grade, timestamp or fact has been invented.
- Learner personal data is minimised.
- Output remains provisional pending authorised human review.
